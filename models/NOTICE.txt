The model is a reproduction of the frozen full-feature HistGradientBoosting
selection (seed 42), fitted only on the original 857 eligible training rows.
It predicts a three-second/two-metre close-interaction proxy on filtered road
vehicle data. It does not predict a validated collision or intervention
probability and is not validated for sidewalk delivery robots or real control.

The original run did not save a weight file. This distribution adds a new
serialization after deterministic reproduction and parity verification; the
original scientific results and model selection remain unchanged.
